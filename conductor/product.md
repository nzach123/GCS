# Initial Concept

Landing page for Game Creator Space (GCS), a college game development club.

# Product Guide - Game Creator Space (GCS)

## Vision
To provide an engaging, fun, and eye-catching entry point for first-year college freshmen interested in game development, encouraging them to join the GCS community and participate in its activities.

## Target Audience
- **Primary:** First-year college freshmen with an interest in game development, ranging from hobbyists to those pursuing it as a career.

## Key Goals
- **Community Growth:** Drive new members to join the official Discord server.
- **Engagement:** Encourage attendance at club meetings, workshops, and speaker events.
- **Inspiration:** Showcase the exciting possibilities of game development through game jams and recruitment for collaborative projects.

## Core Features & Information
- **Community CTAs:** Prominent links to "Join the Discord" and information on how to attend the next meeting.
- **Events Calendar:** A dedicated section listing upcoming speaker events, workshops, and game jams.
- **Game Dev Recruitment:** A call to action focused on the thrill of building games and finding collaborators within the club.

## Aesthetic & Tone
- **Fun & Eye-Catching:** A visual style that captures the creativity of game development. While professional enough for a college club, it should lean into vibrant elements that stand out to students.
