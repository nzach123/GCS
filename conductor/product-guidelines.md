# Product Guidelines - Game Creator Space (GCS)

## Tone of Voice
- **Enthusiastic & Accessible:** High energy and welcoming. We use language that is inviting to beginners and hobbyists while maintaining a professional club atmosphere. 
- **Action-Oriented:** We focus on the "doing"—building games, attending workshops, and joining the community.

## Visual Identity
- **Typography:**
  - **Headlines:** "Bebas Neue" (Bold, impactful).
  - **Body:** "Barlow" (Clean, highly readable).
- **Core Assets:**
  - **Logo:** ssets/gsc-logo.png.
  - **Background:** ssets/Arrow_background.svg.
- **Accessibility Standards:**
  - Minimum color contrast ratio of 4.5:1 for all text elements.
  - Clearly visible focus states for all interactive elements.
  - Semantic HTML structure to ensure screen reader compatibility.
